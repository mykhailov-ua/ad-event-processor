Starting without breaking prod

The fear we hear most: "I'll migrate on Friday and lose the weekend." You do not have to.

  1. Pilot on a separate VPS — 10 days, one server, full click routing. Most teams point a test campaign first, not a full GEO swap on day one.
  2. Import from Keitaro or Binom — 159 integration templates; flows, landers, and postbacks can be operational from day one instead of a six-month rebuild.
  3. License is a file in Settings — paste JWT after install. No vendor account that gates export, no sales call to unlock your own data.
  4. After pilot, upgrade in place — pay USDT, load the new JWT, keep the same deployment. No reinstall, no move back to a cloud tenant.
10 days is enough to see whether you pay more for a cloaker alone than for the license — without a credit card and without a 40-slide sales deck. Message us on Telegram with expected traffic and VPS spec; we send a JWT, not a webinar link.

Questions before the pilot

Will this slow my clicks?

That is a fair worry. Rule filters run on every accepted click; fraud scores are computed in scheduled batch jobs on your analytics store and cached in memory. The hot path does not call an external LLM or pay per request — see Architecture for the ingest budget targets.

I am mid-campaign on Keitaro — do I stop?

No. Import JSON, run the pilot in parallel, duplicate postbacks on a test flow, then switch traffic sources when numbers match. Production Keitaro can keep running until you are comfortable.

Do I need a full-time developer?

You need someone comfortable with basic VPS and Docker — not a dedicated engineer. The onboarding wizard walks through the first campaign; admin console and API cover day-to-day ops.

Is support a ticket queue?

Telegram — support bot or manager. Write deployment_id and what broke. We know postbacks and provider bans do not wait for L1 business hours.

Can my buyer run it without me?

Team RBAC and API keys exist so a junior does not pause production campaigns by mistake — you stay data controller on your metal.

Do you store my campaigns?

No. Everything runs on your server. We have no backend that collects telemetry, campaign configs, or visitor logs.

When things go wrong

Provider ban, dead VPS, war-zone relocation — we know it happens in media buying. For documented force majeure, contact @bidshardsupportbot or @bidshard_manager with your deployment_id and a short description. We can issue additional license activations or reset HWID bindings case by case.

Margin Guard runs on a timer (default 5 minutes), reads Postgres ledger and ClickHouse placement stats, and enqueues an internal routing pause — it does not pause ads in Meta or Google unless you use Enterprise platform sync. Skips when ClickHouse lag exceeds 5 minutes. Silent reject returns 202 to junk traffic without debiting campaign budget. Size VPS per the hardware table; all-in-one under spike + heavy reports risks 503 or OOM.

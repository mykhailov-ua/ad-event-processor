Questions before the pilot

Will this slow down my clicks?

No! Edge rule filters evaluate accepted hits locally in microseconds. Heavy fraud scores run in background batch jobs on your analytics DB. Zero external LLM latency on live traffic.

I'm mid-campaign on Keitaro — should I stop?

No. Import your JSON config, run BidShard in parallel, duplicate postbacks on a test flow, and switch traffic once conversion numbers match.

Do I need a dedicated developer?

No! Full web admin UI is included — campaigns, flows, reports, and Margin Guard in your browser. We help with 1-click install on Telegram.

Is support locked behind ticket queues?

No ticket queues! Direct Telegram support with our bot or manager. We know postbacks and host bans don't wait for L1 business hours.

Can junior buyers ruin live campaigns?

Team RBAC and role management restrict critical campaign actions so junior buyers can't accidentally pause winning adsets.

Do you store my campaign funnels?

No! Everything runs exclusively on your server. We have zero backend endpoints collecting telemetry, funnel configs, or visitor logs.

When a VPS crashes

Server crash, provider ban, or emergency migration — it happens in media buying. Message @bidshardsupportbot or @bidshard_manager on Telegram. We reissue your license key file for your new node fast — case by case, zero hassle.

Margin Guard (Pro tier) monitors campaign ROI on a timer. When profitability drops below your rules, it pauses click routing in the tracker and stops adsets in Meta and Google via API — protecting your budget before the day closes deep red.

Starting without breaking prod

The fear we hear most: "I'll migrate on Friday and lose the weekend." You do not have to.

  1. Pilot on a separate VPS — 10 days, one server, full click routing. Most teams point a test campaign first, not a full GEO swap on day one.
  2. Import from Keitaro or Binom — 159 integration templates; flows, landers, and postbacks can be operational from day one instead of a six-month rebuild.
  3. License is a file in Settings — paste it after install. No vendor account that gates export, no sales call to unlock your own data.
  4. After pilot, upgrade in place — pay USDT, load the new license file, keep the same deployment. No reinstall, no move back to a cloud tenant.
10 days is enough to see whether you pay more for a cloaker alone than for the license — without a credit card and without a 40-slide sales deck. Message us on Telegram with expected traffic and VPS spec; we send a license file, not a webinar link.

Questions before the pilot

Will this slow my clicks?

That is a fair worry. Rule filters run on every accepted click; fraud scores are computed in scheduled batch jobs on your analytics store and cached for live routing. The hot path does not call an external LLM or pay per request.

I am mid-campaign on Keitaro — do I stop?

No. Import JSON, run the pilot in parallel, duplicate postbacks on a test flow, then switch traffic sources when numbers match. Production Keitaro can keep running until you are comfortable.

Do I need a full-time developer?

You need someone comfortable with basic VPS and Docker — not a dedicated engineer. The onboarding wizard walks through the first campaign; admin console covers day-to-day campaign work.

Is support a ticket queue?

Telegram — support bot or manager. Describe what broke and which server you moved to. We know postbacks and provider bans do not wait for L1 business hours.

Can my buyer run it without me?

Team roles and API keys exist so a junior does not pause production campaigns by mistake — you stay data controller on your metal.

Do you store my campaigns?

No. Everything runs on your server. We have no backend that collects telemetry, campaign configs, or visitor logs.

When things go wrong

Dead VPS, provider shutdown, or an emergency move to new hardware — it happens in media buying. Contact @bidshardsupportbot or @bidshard_manager on Telegram with a short description of what happened. We can send a new license key for your replacement server — reviewed case by case, not treated as abuse.

Margin Guard (Pro tier) watches ledger totals and placement stats on a timer. When ROI drops below your rules, it pauses routing inside the tracker and can pause linked campaigns in Meta and Google — so spend stops bleeding while you fix the funnel, not after the day ends red.

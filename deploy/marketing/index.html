Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
Why doesn't my tracker match the CPA network?
Usually status timing (hold vs confirmed), a bad subid in the postback URL, or a gap on the network side. Pro tier logs every postback hop so you see where the chain broke — before you argue in screenshots.
Do I still need a cloaker?
Built-in cloaking — most teams drop a separate cloaker bill. Test on the pilot in heavy-moderation GEOs and compare block rates.
Can I run the pilot parallel to Keitaro?
Yes — that is what we recommend. Import JSON, duplicate postbacks on a test flow, keep Keitaro live until numbers match, then switch sources.
Do you store my campaigns?
No. Everything runs exclusively on your server. We have no backend API that collects telemetry, campaign configurations, or visitor logs.
How does this compare to Keitaro or Binom?
Same deployment model — self-hosted on your VPS, your traffic never leaves your infrastructure. The difference is one stack: routing, cloaking, antifraud, cost sync, and CAPI without separate tracker, cloaker, and IVT bills. Import JSON from Keitaro or Binom and run a parallel pilot.
What happens after the pilot?
Pay USDT for your matching tier. We generate and send a new license file. You load it into your existing deployment with no reinstall needed.
What if I lose a server or need to relocate?
Provider ban or dead VPS? Message @bidshardsupportbot or @bidshard_manager on Telegram — we reissue your license key for the new server.
Do I need to be a developer?
You need comfort with basic VPS and Docker — not a full-time developer. The onboarding wizard walks you through the first campaign.
How do I start?
Reach out on Telegram to obtain a pilot license. Execute the single-line install script on your host, activate the license in your browser, and you are live.

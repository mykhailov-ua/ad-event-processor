Tracker Architecture

How it works

BidShard is self-hosted software deployed on your VPS or dedicated server. Your traffic and funnels stay strictly on your metal — we ship a high-RPS stack and offline license key file.

On this page

Your server

A single installation runs click routing, campaign web admin, budget accounting, and columnar analytics on hardware under your control. Visitors hit your domain; valid hits route instantly without proxying through third-party data centers.

Ingress traffic

Your ads route clicks to tracker URLs. Click routing, landers, and offer flows run locally with zero cloud proxy lag.

Web admin & control

Campaign management, team RBAC roles, ad spend sync, and alert rules — all in one browser UI.

100% data privacy

Funnel configs, click logs, and reports live on your server. We never phone home or harvest your data.

Edge filtering before budget spend

Every incoming click passes edge rule checks — GEO, device signals, timing, bot filters, and cloaking — before daily budget is debited. Bots and reviewer crawlers get a clean decoy view while your daily cap is preserved for money traffic.

Built-in cloaking. No extra SaaS bill — bot and moderator filtering operates right inside the tracker engine.

Deep ML antifraud runs background scoring on your analytics store — avoiding per-click API costs and latency.

Reports, CAPI & Postback Trail

Accepted conversions are logged to your columnar analytics DB for instant reports and dispute exports. S2S postbacks fire to affiliate networks with full hop-by-hop logging so you verify exact postback delivery.

Cost sync imports spend from ad networks every 15 minutes for real-time ROI tracking. Server-side Conversion APIs (Meta, Google, TikTok) fire directly from your server to train network bidding on real signal.

Offline license key, zero phone-home

Your plan tier and feature flags reside in an offline license key file loaded via the web admin UI. Verification runs locally on your VPS — no phone-home call per click, no vendor lockouts.

Upgrades are delivered as a fresh key file — load it in your browser with zero downtime. Lost a server node? Message us on Telegram — we reissue your license key file fast.
